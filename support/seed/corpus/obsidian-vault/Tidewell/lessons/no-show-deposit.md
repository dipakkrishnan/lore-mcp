# No-show deposit

A 25 % no-show deposit cut no-shows from 18 % to 4 % in six weeks. Delia tried it first, on new customers only, and the ones who paid the deposit almost never cancel late. The deposit is charged through Stripe Connect Express when the booking is made and credited against the final bill.
