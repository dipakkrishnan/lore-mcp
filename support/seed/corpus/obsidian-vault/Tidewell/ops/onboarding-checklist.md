# <Onboarding> 🐕 checklist & "quotes"

Steps for a new groomer, in order, before their first booking goes live:

- [ ] Connect Stripe Connect Express and read the payout warning aloud.
- [ ] Add the van, the service area and the working hours.
- [ ] Import the customer list from a spreadsheet, or start empty.
- [ ] Send one test booking to a phone the groomer owns.
