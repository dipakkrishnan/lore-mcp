How Tidewell started

Tidewell started in January 2026 with a spreadsheet and a phone call to Delia, who ran a one-van grooming shop in Port Alder and kept her bookings on a paper calendar.

By March she was the first paying customer, and the spreadsheet had become a booking form with a route on the back of it.
