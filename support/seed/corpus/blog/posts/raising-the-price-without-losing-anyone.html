Raising the price without losing anyone

Raising $29 to $39 with grandfathering lost nobody; the new price only ever applied to people who never saw the old one.

On 1 August the monthly price went from $29 to $39. Every groomer who was already paying kept $29 for as long as they stay. New signups see only $39, so nobody is ever told that they are paying more than someone else.

The email I sent to the forty existing groomers was four sentences long, and two of them replied with a thank-you.
