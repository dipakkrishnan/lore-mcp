Routes & ramps: <van> life 🚐

A day in a grooming van is mostly ramps, parking and low bridges, and the software only sees the addresses.
Ramp angles decide which dogs can be booked into which van.
Low bridges decide which streets the route optimiser may use.
Parking decides whether the next stop starts on time.
