## Problem

A fixed price can't respond to demand. A piece that sells well stays
underpriced, and one that never sells stays where it is.

## Proposed approach

Unclear, needs investigation. It comes after per-piece prices exist. A likely
first cut is suggestions built on the sales and view counts the node already
records ("this sold 20 copies this week, consider $X"), before any automatic
repricing. Any price change has to move every payment rail together (MON-028).
