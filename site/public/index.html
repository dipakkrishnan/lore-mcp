3 · Sell, if you want

You choose what to answer

Answer a bid, or share something you think is useful. Lore drafts a short piece. You read it, set a price and approve it. Lore never holds the money: it goes straight to you.

Questions

Is this selling my data?
No. Lore never sells facts about you or your chats. It sells short pieces you approve, one at a time.
Is Lore an AI assistant?
No. Lore works next to the AI apps you already use. It keeps what you learn in them, privately, on your Mac.
Who buys, and why?
People and their AI agents who need firsthand experience they can't find online. They read a free sample before they pay.
